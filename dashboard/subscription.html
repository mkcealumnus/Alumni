Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference. When downgrading, your new plan will take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for Enterprise plans.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial for the Pro plan. No credit card required to start your trial.

What happens if I cancel?

If you cancel, you'll continue to have access to your current plan until the end of your billing period. After that, your account will be downgraded to the Free plan.
