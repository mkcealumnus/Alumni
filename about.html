About SignBridgeAI

Pioneering inclusive communication through artificial intelligence

Our Mission

SignBridgeAI is a pioneering technology company dedicated to breaking down communication barriers between the deaf and hearing communities. Founded with a vision to create inclusive communication tools, we leverage advanced artificial intelligence and machine learning to develop innovative sign language solutions.

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to ensure that no one is left behind in communication. We believe that everyone deserves the right to express themselves and be understood, regardless of how they communicate.

Our Story

2020 - The Beginning

SignBridgeAI was founded by a team of AI researchers and accessibility advocates who recognized the need for better sign language technology.

2021 - First Prototype

We launched our first prototype, achieving 85% accuracy in ASL recognition, marking a significant breakthrough in the field.

2022 - Global Expansion

Expanded support to 10 sign languages and launched our mobile app, reaching users in over 50 countries.

2023 - Enterprise Launch

Introduced SignBridge Enterprise, bringing accessibility solutions to Fortune 500 companies and government organizations.

2024 - 99% Accuracy

Achieved industry-leading 99% accuracy with our AI models, supporting 15+ sign languages and 10,000+ active users.
