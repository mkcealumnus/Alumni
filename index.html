About Us

SignBridgeAI is a pioneering technology company dedicated to breaking down communication barriers between the deaf and hearing communities. Founded with a vision to create inclusive communication tools, we leverage advanced artificial intelligence and machine learning to develop innovative sign language solutions.

Our team of AI researchers, linguists, and accessibility advocates work tirelessly to create technology that recognizes, interprets, and translates sign language in real-time, making communication seamless for everyone.
