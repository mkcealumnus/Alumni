Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is SignBridgeAI's translation?

Our AI achieves 99% accuracy for common signs and phrases. Accuracy may vary for regional dialects and specialized vocabulary, but we're constantly improving.

Which sign languages do you support?

We currently support 15+ sign languages including ASL, BSL, Auslan, LSF, DGS, JSL, and more. Check our documentation for the full list.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! Our mobile app offers a free tier with basic features. Enterprise and API customers can request a 14-day free trial.

How is my data protected?

We use end-to-end encryption and are GDPR/HIPAA compliant. Video data is processed in real-time and never stored without explicit consent.

Can I use SignBridgeAI offline?

Our mobile app includes offline mode with a downloadable language pack. Some advanced features require an internet connection.

How do I integrate the API?

Our API comes with comprehensive documentation, SDK libraries for popular languages, and a sandbox environment. Most integrations take less than a day.
